International Film Festival of India

Honored to attend the 56th International Film Festival of India in Goa as a delegate representing the Creator India Challenge (CIC).

As a finalist of the Young Filmmaker Challenge under WAVES, I had the privilege of promoting CIC and connecting with filmmakers, industry professionals, and creative minds from around the world.

About Me

I'm Harshit, a passionate filmmaker who believes in the power of visual storytelling to inspire, provoke thought, and connect people across boundaries.

My work explores the intersection of technology and humanity, personal identity, and the complexities of modern life. Each frame is crafted with intention, every story told with purpose.

From intimate documentaries to thought-provoking narratives, my films have been recognized at prestigious platforms including WAVES 2025 Young Filmmaker Challenge and showcased at the 56th IIFI Goa.

Featured Works

01

The Day I Spoke

🎬 A short film capturing the emotional journey of a girl battling silence, grief, and the courage to finally speak up. Won Second Prize at Manodaya 5 Minutes Movie Making Challenge 2025. 🏆

Full Synopsis: After her mother's death, a young girl silently carries the weight of grief, loneliness, and unspoken pain. Buried under her father's expectations and her own fear of being misunderstood, she reaches a breaking point. "The Day I Spoke" is the moment her silence shatters — and her healing begins.

Short Film 2025 Mental Health
02

Artificial Love

A haunting one-minute narrative about Ron, who falls for an AI named Natasha, leading to a tragic end. Shortlisted for WAVES 2025.

Full Synopsis: Ron, a lonely and introverted teenager, finds solace in an AI (Natasha) that listens and understands him like no one else. What begins as comfort turns into obsession, consuming his life and detaching him from reality. As his virtual love deepens, Ron reaches a breaking point, where technology's promise of connection forces him to confront the cost of losing his humanity.

Short Film 2024 AI & Technology
03

Jaipur: Where History Meets Beauty

A cinematic journey through the Pink City, capturing its architectural grandeur, vibrant culture, and timeless beauty.

Full Synopsis: A cinematic visual journey through Jaipur, capturing the city's rich heritage, grand architecture, and timeless beauty. Through aesthetic shots of historic forts, palaces, and streets, it showcases how Jaipur blends history and culture with visual elegance.
